Academic Website

I am a mathematician interested in algebraic and arithmetic geometry. Currently, I am a Research Fellow at St John's College and a member of the number theory group at DPMMS, University of Cambridge.

My research focuses on the arithmetic and geometry of curves and abelian varieties. Here is my CV (updated March 2026): [pdf]
